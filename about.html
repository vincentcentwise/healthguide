ABOUT HEALTHGUIDE

Making health information easier to discover.

HealthGuide is a health-information and first-aid resource platform designed to help people discover educational health resources, medicine information, first-aid guidance and healthcare-related resources in one accessible place.

Explore Medicines Explore First Aid
OUR MISSION

Information should be easier to understand and easier to find.

HealthGuide was created to bring useful health-information resources together in a simple and accessible digital experience.

Instead of searching across many different resources, users can use HealthGuide to discover medicine information, learn basic first-aid concepts and find healthcare-related resources.

Our goal is to make health education more accessible while encouraging users to seek qualified professional care whenever it is necessary.

IMPORTANT

HealthGuide is an educational resource.

HealthGuide does not diagnose medical conditions, prescribe medication or replace qualified healthcare professionals.

Information presented on the platform should be used for educational purposes. Users should consult an appropriately qualified healthcare professional for personal medical concerns.

In an emergency or life-threatening situation, contact your local emergency medical service immediately.

PRIVACY & RESPONSIBILITY

Designed with responsible health technology in mind.

HealthGuide is being designed to minimize unnecessary collection of sensitive personal health information.

Account features are intended to help users organize their HealthGuide experience, such as saved resources, search history and preferences.

HealthGuide is not intended to function as a medical-record system.
